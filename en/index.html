Studio

Seven fictional brand concepts, not live shops.

Peninsula Studio creates clickable ecommerce concepts to test a brand’s visual direction, product presentation and customer journey before building the live store. These seven fictional projects show different approaches across retail categories.

What these projects show

Five projects demonstrate Shopify-style purchase journeys. Wagmorrow explores quick view and a shopping bag; Liora explores a motion-led hero and a jewellery wish list. None has a live backend or real payment processing.

How we work

A straightforward route from the first conversation to handover.

  1. Understand your business

    We discuss your products, audience and the point where customers hesitate.

  2. Design the journey

    For Growth and Brand-led stores, we make key pages clickable before the Shopify build. Starter begins with an existing theme; Local Presence is a single-page website.

  3. Build in Shopify

    For store packages, we set up the agreed theme, products and apps in your Shopify account.

  4. Hand over

    You receive guidance for updating the store. Revision rounds and post-launch support are agreed in the proposal.

Client feedback

Frequently asked questions

Are these live Shopify stores?

No. These are static concepts and clickable demos. They have no Shopify admin or live payments. A real store build is a separate project.

Can you build a multilingual store?

Yes, if included in the agreed scope. These demos do not themselves include language switching.

What do I need to prepare?

Your products, approximate prices, target customers and any existing brand assets. We can help shape a direction if you do not have a brand guide.

How do revisions and post-launch changes work?

Each package lists its revision rounds and period for fixing issues within the agreed scope. One consolidated set of feedback counts as a round. New pages or features are quoted separately.
