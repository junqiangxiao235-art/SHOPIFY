山月

Our story

SHANYUE is a small jewelry workshop for women who want gold they can actually wear. Not a vault piece. Not a costume. Something that survives the commute, the dinner, and the gift you are still thinking about.

The founder started the bench in Baiyun District, Guangzhou, after years of making pieces that were either too precious to wear or too light to trust. Baiyun Workshop sits between those two. Vermeil for every day. Solid 14k and small lab diamonds when the piece should last.

Baiyun Workshop

Four people, one room, four steps. Nothing is rushed through a third-party factory line in this story. Modelling, casting, setting, and a last check all happen at the bench.

The meaning of 山月

山间明月

山月 is the moon over the mountains: 山间明月. Not the moon as a poster, and not a logo shouting from across the room. It is the light you notice between the peaks, close enough to wear. The seal on the site is that name, kept small on purpose.
