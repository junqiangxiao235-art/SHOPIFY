Care

Care

Vermeil is a layer of 18k gold over sterling silver. It is made to be worn, and the layer is 2.5 µm on our pieces. Daily wear will soften the shine over years. That is the life of the metal, not a fault.

  • Put jewelry on after perfume, lotion, and hairspray.
  • Take it off for swimming, gyms, and the shower.
  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Skip dips and polishing cloths made for plated fashion jewelry.
  • Store pieces separately so chains do not knot and stones do not knock.
  • 14k solid gold and lab diamonds can follow the same routine. They simply wear more slowly.

If a clasp fails or a stone loosens within two years, see the warranty.
