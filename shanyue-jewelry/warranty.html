Warranty

Every piece is covered for two years from the day it ships. The warranty covers a broken clasp, a stone that falls out of its setting, and a fault in how the piece was made.

It does not cover loss, theft, or the slow softening of vermeil from daily wear. A 2.5 µm gold layer is meant to be lived in. If you want metal that does not plate-wear, choose 14k on Zodiac or a Lantern piece.

Returns are accepted within 30 days in original condition. Engraved pendants are final sale once the letters are cut, because we cannot put the blank back. You can still use the warranty if the setting or clasp fails.

This is a demo policy for a fictional brand. No repairs are actually offered.
