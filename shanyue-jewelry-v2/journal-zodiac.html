What the twelve zodiac animals mean

The Chinese zodiac is a twelve-year cycle. People use the animal of a birth year as a shorthand for character, and as a gift. The animal can shift for births in January or February, because the lunar new year does not fall on 1 January. Our finder uses the calendar year only and says so.

  • Rat — quick, resourceful.
  • Ox — steady, patient.
  • Tiger — bold.
  • Rabbit — gentle, alert.
  • Dragon — the sign people ask for when a gift should feel lucky.
  • Snake — calm, observant.
  • Horse — restless, in motion.
  • Goat — soft-spoken, loyal.
  • Monkey — playful, quick.
  • Rooster — precise, bright.
  • Dog — loyal, plain-spoken.
  • Pig — warm, generous.
