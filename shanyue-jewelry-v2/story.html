Factory owner. Sample: 18 years of OEM work in Dongguan, mostly vermeil chains and small stone setting. No awards, no store list. The specialty is everyday weight: pieces light enough to keep on.

Why vermeil and lab diamonds

Vermeil gives a gold surface without the price of solid gold, so the piece gets worn. Lab diamonds are brighter in small sizes, lighter on the ear, and a fairer price than a mined stone of the same look. That is the reason for the materials, not a medal.
